Terms of use

Use the bot fairly. Keep rights to the music you send.

Last updated 14 September 2026.

MetaMusic is a Telegram bot that tags music files and can save them to your Google Drive. It is offered as-is, mainly for people the operator has invited. Access can be refused or blacklisted at any time. How to use it: manual.

Your files

You must have the right to send and store the audio you submit. Using the bot does not give you a license to anyone else’s recordings. You are responsible for copyright, and for Telegram’s and Google’s rules.

Google Drive is optional

You can use tagging without connecting Drive. If you do connect it, the bot can write into folders it creates in your account. It still cannot read the rest of your Drive. Unlink in the Mini App when you want that to stop. Google’s own terms also apply to your Google account.

How long things last

Music we still have on our servers is deleted after one week. If you do not use the bot for three months, we forget your stored session and Google login. Copies already in your Drive stay until you delete them there. The privacy policy explains this in full.

The Mini App

Login, settings, review, and suggest screens run inside Telegram at https://music.avje.in. Do not paste OAuth or login URLs in a public group. Those links are one-time and meant only for you.

No warranty

Tags can be wrong. Files can disappear because of the one-week cleanup, an unlink, or an admin action. The bot can go down. We are not liable for lost files, bad tags, or Drive quota.

Please don’t

Do not hammer search, upload, or the Mini App past the rate limits. Do not try to steal tokens, sessions, or other people’s files. The operator can drop a job, blacklist a user, or shut the bot off.
